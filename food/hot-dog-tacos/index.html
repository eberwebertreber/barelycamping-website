Hot Dog Tacos

1.2 out of 10
  • Dinner
  • Over the fire

This was my buddy Tyler's idea. Taco Bell taco shells, hot dogs and whatever condiments you have. You cook the dogs over the fire and throw them in a shell. We might have joked about it in the video, but it was terrible. I would not recommend this. It's a rent is due tomorrow and you have no money for groceries kind of meal.

I don't like how it doesn't even fit in the whole shell.

What you need

Gear

How to make it

  1. Cook the hot dogs over the fire.
  2. Pull them off and let them cool for a minute.
  3. Put a hot dog in a taco shell and add your condiments.
