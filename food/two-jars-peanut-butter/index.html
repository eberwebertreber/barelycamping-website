Two Jars of Peanut Butter for 24 Hours

0.0 out of 10
  • Snack
  • No cook

This was a Temu challenge where two jars of peanut butter were my only food for 24 hours. They cost $16.16. My thinking was it's a ton of fat and calories, so if I ate half a jar before bed my body would be processing it all night and keeping me warm. Peanut butter tastes good, just not at this quantity. Half a jar in, my stomach was killing me. I left both jars hanging in a tree with the spoon.

I'm never gonna eat peanut butter ever again.
