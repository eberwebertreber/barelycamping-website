Everything Bagel Mac

9.1 out of 10
  • Dinner
  • Pot

This is a little specialty I do. Regular boxed mac and cheese, then everything bagel seasoning and some hot sauce on top. Mine came out a little more cooked than I like because I left it on the fire forever, and it was still S tier. It's nostalgic for me and it's just so good. If you try it, DM me a picture on Instagram and tell me what you think.

Oh my lord.

What you need

Gear

How to make it

  1. Boil the noodles in a pot on the grate.
  2. Drain, then stir in butter, the cheese packet and a splash of milk.
  3. Top with everything bagel seasoning and hot sauce.
