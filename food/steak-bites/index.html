Foil Packet Steak Bites and Potatoes

7.1 out of 10
  • Dinner
  • Foil

The last time I tried steak and potatoes it did not work out, so this was the redo. I seasoned the steak with chipotle sea salt and wrapped it in foil with Kerrygold butter, and the steak turned out amazing. The potatoes were the problem again. I put about nine baby potatoes on with the steak and they came out raw, so next time they go on the coals way earlier.

The steak turned out amazing. The potatoes are not cooked all the way.

What you need

Gear

How to make it

  1. Rinse the potatoes and get them on the coals in foil first. Mine went on with the steak and came out raw.
  2. Cut the steak into bites, season it, and wrap it in foil with a slice of butter.
  3. Set the steak on the coals for the last few minutes, then cut one open to check it.
