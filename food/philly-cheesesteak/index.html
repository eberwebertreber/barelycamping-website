Campfire Philly Cheesesteak

4.7 out of 10
  • Dinner
  • Cast iron

A cheesesteak is not a good idea when you're backcountry camping. I blended the steak in a blender before I even got there, so it was all tiny little pieces, and they scattered everywhere. It took me over an hour to make, and there was steak all over the campsite. I ended up leaving in the middle of the night because I was too scared of animals coming to eat it all.

This Philly cheesesteak has taken me over an hour.

What you need

Gear

How to make it

  1. Toast the bread in the cast iron first.
  2. Cook the steak in the cast iron over the fire.
  3. Lay a slice of provolone on top, let it melt, and pile it on the bread.
