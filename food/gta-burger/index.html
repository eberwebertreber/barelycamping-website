Cast Iron GTA Burger

7.7 out of 10
  • Dinner
  • Cast iron

GTA 6 came out the day I filmed this, and there's a burger place all over the gameplay where the most popular thing on the menu is some crazy stacked smash burger. I wasn't going to make that, but I wanted dinner inspired by it. I forgot my cast iron at home, so I was cooking on a brand new one, and the patties came out pretty broken up, kind of like a ground beef sandwich. A pound of grass-fed beef, two burgers. The only thing it has that my smash burgers didn't is cheese and lettuce. It's a good burger for sure. Maybe I should have reviewed it before I ate the second one.

It's a good burger, for sure.

What you need

Gear

How to make it

  1. Let the fire burn down to coals and set the cast iron on top. Mine weren't hot enough, so it cooked slower.
  2. Brown the onions, then throw the beef on top of them so the onions get smashed into it.
  3. Season it and split it into two burgers.
  4. Build it on the brioche with a Kraft Single, lettuce and the onions, then add your mayo or ketchup.
