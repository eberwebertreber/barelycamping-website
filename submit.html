Let's Go Camping

Submit a video of yourself saying "let's go camping" to be featured in an upcoming BarelyCamping video.

How to record your video

  1. Record somewhere you can be heard clearly.
  2. Hold your phone horizontally. Vertical videos will not be used.
  3. Say your name, where you're from, then say "let's go camping."
  4. Keep the video under 15 seconds.

Example: "I'm Dylan, I'm from Ohio, let's go camping."
