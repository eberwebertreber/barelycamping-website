Privacy

BarelyCamping · updated August 2026

Short version: this is just a landing page for my YouTube channel. I don't collect your personal info, I don't sell anything to anyone, and there are no ads on this site. I do count visits so I know whether anyone's actually out here.

What this site does

It shows links to my socials, pulls in my latest YouTube videos, and has a little camping game. That's it. There's no account, no login, no contact form, no email signup.

Stuff stored on your own device

Your browser saves one small thing locally so the site behaves: a visitor number, so your camper number stays the same when you come back. That stays on your device. I never see it. Clear your browser data and it's gone.

Visitor count

To give each person a "camper #" the site pings a simple public counter. It just bumps a number up by one. No names, no emails, nothing that points back to you.

Analytics

I use Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics to see how many people visit, which pages they land on, and roughly what country they're in. Google Analytics sets a cookie so it can tell a repeat visit from a new one. Vercel's version doesn't use cookies at all. Neither one tells me who you are, and I never see anything that points back to you personally.

If you'd rather not be counted, most ad blockers stop both of them, and the site works exactly the same either way.

Advertising

I also run the Meta Pixel, the tracking tool behind Facebook and Instagram ads. It tells Meta that a browser visited this site, which lets me show ads later to people who have been here before. Mostly that's for merch, whenever that finally launches.

Worth being clear about what this does and doesn't do: I never learn who you are. I can't pull your name or your email out of it, and I can't download a list of anyone who visited. All it gives me is the ability to point an ad at a group of people I'll never see.

Most ad blockers stop it, same as the analytics, and nothing on the site breaks if you block it.

Other companies

The site is hosted on Vercel and the videos come from YouTube, so those services see normal web traffic the way every website does (basically your IP and browser, for the request to work). I don't get any of that, and you can read their own policies if you want the details.

Kids

This isn't aimed at collecting info from anyone, kids included, because I'm not collecting info, period.
